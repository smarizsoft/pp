About ExamSearch Pakistan

Simplifying board exam preparation for Matric and Intermediate students across Pakistan.

Our Mission

Every year, hundreds of thousands of students appearing in Karachi Board (BSEK/BIEK), Federal Board (FBISE), and Punjab Boards spend hours searching through scattered books and websites for past paper solutions. ExamSearch Pakistan was created to solve this problem by bringing repeated exam questions, year-wise frequency data, written explanations, diagrams, PDFs, and video lectures together in one fast, distraction-free search portal.

Key Features of Our Platform

  • Smart Criteria Filtering: Filter questions instantly by your exact Educational Board, Class, and Subject.
  • No Duplicate Clutter: Repeated questions are consolidated into a single card showing all the exam years they appeared in.
  • Multi-Format Solutions: View text notes, high-resolution diagrams, PDF handouts, and YouTube video explanations in a single popup without page reloads.
