About ExamSearch Pakistan

ExamSearch Pakistan is an educational search portal designed to help Matric (9th & 10th) and Intermediate (11th & 12th) students prepare effectively for their annual board examinations.

Our Mission

Every year, thousands of students across Karachi Board (BSEK/BIEK), Federal Board (FBISE), and Punjab Boards spend hours looking for organized past paper solutions. Our mission is to simplify exam revision by consolidating repeated questions by exam year alongside clear written explanations, labeled diagrams, PDF notes, and video lectures.

What Makes Us Different

  • Board-Specific Filtering: Quickly narrow down questions according to your exact Board, Class, and Subject.
  • Year-Wise Frequency: See every year a question appeared in board papers without duplicate clutter.
  • All-in-One Popups: View text answers, diagrams, PDF handouts, and video explanations on a single screen without refreshing the page.
